# Core Concepts

This section covers the core concepts you need to understand to effectively use our platform.

## Agents

Agents are the autonomous units that perform tasks. They can be configured with specific models, tools, and instructions.

## Workflows

Workflows allow you to chain multiple agents and tools together to perform complex tasks.

## Tools

Tools are the capabilities you give to your agents, such as searching the web, reading files, or executing code.
